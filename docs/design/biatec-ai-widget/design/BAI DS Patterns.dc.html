Risk review

Ordinary risk is explained on a neutral surface. Only consequential risk gets a warning treatment.

Standard · neutral surface
Crypto prices can move 5–10% in a day. A $100 position could lose about $10 in an ordinary move.
Elevated · warning
This order uses 84% of today's allocation.
Consequential · danger
Slippage would exceed your 0.5% cap in current liquidity. Approval is blocked until you edit the order.
