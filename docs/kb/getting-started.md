# Getting started with Biatec Chat

## Step 1: just ask

Open Biatec AI with the green button in the bottom-right corner of Biatec Web or Biatec DEX. You can ask about Biatec products, Algorand, decentralised exchanges, fees and how to get started without an account. As a guest you can also ask for a 24-hour market snapshot of an asset pair on Biatec DEX.

Examples to try:

- "Explain to me what the blockchain is and why Biatec uses Algorand."
- "How does a Biatec Safe work?"
- "What does a swap on Biatec DEX cost?"

## Step 2: sign in

To act on your own funds, select Sign in in the chat. A popup opens where you sign in with your Google or Microsoft account through Biatec OIDC. The first time, Biatec OIDC creates your self-custody wallet: Algorand, EVM and Bitcoin addresses whose keys are stored encrypted in your own cloud storage. The chat continues where you left off when the popup closes.

## Step 3: fund your address

Every Algorand account needs a small minimum balance of ALGO before it can be used, and a little more for each asset it holds. Send some ALGO to your Algorand address first. Ask "What are my addresses?" to see them, and "What is my balance?" to check.

## Step 4: ask for an action

Signed-in users can ask, for example:

- "Swap 50 USDC to ALGO."
- "Transfer 10 ALGO to my secondary address."
- "Add liquidity to the Biatec DEX EURS/USDC pool."
- "Create a Biatec Safe for my team with 2-of-3 approvals."
- "What are my current spending limits?"

The assistant prepares an action preview card that shows exactly what will happen. Open Review, check the details, then select Confirm.

## Step 5: authorise signing (once per session)

The first time an action needs a signature in a session, a popup asks you to grant signing permission to Biatec Chat. You grant it once per session. Signing still happens only within your own spending limits.

## Step 6: approve larger actions on your phone

If an action is valued above your approval threshold (1000 EUR by default), or you chose to approve everything, the widget shows a short code and waits for your phone. Open Biatec Authenticator, check that the amount, asset and destination match, type the code and approve. Install and enrol the app ahead of time: a newly enrolled first device becomes active after 24 hours.

## Step 7: keep the receipt

When the action completes, the card shows the transaction ID with a link to a blockchain explorer, and a Download evidence link for the session's signed record.
