# Market data

## Market snapshot

Anyone, signed in or not, can ask Biatec AI for a market snapshot of an asset pair, for example "Show me the ALGO/USDC market". The assistant shows a card with:

- the current price of the pair;
- the 24-hour change with an arrow, sign and word;
- the 24-hour high and low;
- the 24-hour trading volume;
- a small sparkline chart;
- a stamp "Snapshot HH:MM UTC · not live".

The data comes from public Biatec DEX trade data and is cached for a short time.

## Facts only

The market snapshot is a static snapshot, never a live or animated price. It comes without forecasts, trading signals or recommendations. Biatec AI doesn't tell you whether to buy, sell or hold.

## When data is unavailable

If the market data source doesn't respond, the assistant says that market data is unavailable instead of guessing.

## Your own holdings

Signed-in users can ask "What is my balance?" for a holdings card: the total, an allocation bar, one row per asset, and a note when one asset makes up most of the portfolio.
