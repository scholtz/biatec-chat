# Glossary

**ALGO**: the native coin of the Algorand blockchain, used for fees and minimum balances.

**Algorand Standard Asset (ASA)**: a token on Algorand with a numeric asset ID, such as USDC or EURS.

**AMM (automated market maker)**: an exchange that prices trades from the assets held in a pool rather than an order book.

**Action preview**: the card Biatec AI shows before anything is signed, with the exact amounts, destination, fees, limits and approval needs.

**Agent mode**: what Biatec AI may do in a session: Analyze only, Prepare for approval, or Paused.

**Approval threshold**: the value above which an action needs phone approval; 1000 EUR by default.

**Binding code**: the short code shown in the chat that you type into Biatec Authenticator to approve (number matching).

**Biatec Authenticator**: the mobile app that approves high-value actions with a device-bound key.

**Biatec Consent Protocol**: the open protocol that lets any bank or app ask a Biatec user for phone approval.

**Biatec OIDC**: Biatec's sign-in service and self-custody wallet.

**Biatec MCP**: the service that gives AI assistants wallet tools over the Model Context Protocol.

**Biatec Router**: Biatec's swap routing, compared with other aggregators for the best result.

**Biatec Safe / Algo Safe**: a shared smart account controlled by M-of-N approvals.

**CIBA**: Client-Initiated Backchannel Authentication, the OpenID standard behind phone approvals.

**Concentrated liquidity**: liquidity placed in a chosen price range rather than across all prices.

**Cooling-off**: the rule that a new app's actions, and a user's first action through an app, need phone approval.

**Evidence record**: the signed, hash-chained log of a conversation, exportable and verifiable.

**Fee tier**: the trading fee percentage of a DEX pool, paid to liquidity providers.

**Group hash**: the fingerprint of an exact transaction group that binds the preview, approval and signature together.

**Guest (anonymous tier)**: using Biatec AI without signing in; questions and market data only.

**LP token**: a token representing a liquidity provider's share of a pool.

**Mainnet / Testnet**: the real Algorand network and the practice network whose tokens have no value.

**Minimum balance**: ALGO an Algorand account must keep, growing with each asset opt-in.

**Number matching**: typing a code from the requesting screen into the phone, so requests you didn't start can't be approved.

**OpenID Federation**: a standard for verifying an organisation's identity through a chain of trust.

**Opt-in**: a zero-amount transfer that lets an Algorand account receive an asset.

**Prompt injection**: hidden instructions in content that try to manipulate an AI; Biatec Chat treats such content as data.

**Self-custody**: the user, not a company, controls the keys to their funds.

**Sign scope**: the permission granted once per session that lets Biatec Chat request signatures.

**Slippage**: the difference between the quoted and executed price; your setting caps it.

**Spending limits**: daily, weekly and monthly caps on your wallet, enforced by Biatec OIDC.

**Tenant**: an organisation running Biatec Chat under its own domain and settings.

**USDC / EURS**: US dollar and euro stablecoins available as Algorand Standard Assets.

**x402**: an HTTP payment standard that lets agents pay for resources automatically.
