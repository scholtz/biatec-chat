Logo concepts

Three flat, geometric directions in the Biatec DEX greens. The shared idea: intelligence as a dependable mechanism, not a mystery.

Concept A
Intelligent gear
An AI chip held inside a gear: the platform and its rules around the intelligence. Friendly, but the gear adds detail that gets lost at small sizes.
Concept BSelected
Bare chip
One solid Biatec-green chip with a bold AI label and short pins. It is the most direct and most legible at every size, from a 16 px favicon to a launcher.
Concept C
Segmented ring
An abstract gear made from ring segments around a core. Elegant, but at small sizes it loses the AI cue and reads as a loading spinner.

Intelligence you can read at a glance.

A single Biatec DEX green chip with a bold, dark AI label and short pins. Below 24 px the pins drop and the chip grows to fill the frame, so "AI" stays readable in every workspace. The wordmark keeps Biatec's script lettering beside a solid AI tag.
