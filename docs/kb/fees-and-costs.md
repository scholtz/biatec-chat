# Fees and costs

## Does Biatec Chat cost anything?

Asking Biatec AI questions is free. When you act, you pay only the blockchain fees and any trading fees, all shown in the preview before you confirm.

## Algorand network fees

The minimum Algorand fee is 0.001 ALGO per transaction. A transaction group pays the sum of its transactions' fees. Calls to smart contracts that call other contracts may need a little extra fee. A failed transaction still pays its fee.

## Minimum balance is not a fee

Algorand reserves 0.1 ALGO per account and 0.1 ALGO per asset opt-in as a minimum balance. It's locked, not spent; opting out of an asset releases it.

## Biatec DEX trading fees

Each Biatec DEX pool has a fee tier set when it was created. A swap pays that percentage of the traded amount to the pool's liquidity providers. The fee tier appears on the pool and in the swap preview.

## Slippage is a cost too

The worst-case price you accept is shown as the minimum amount you will receive. Your actual result is at least that, or the swap fails.

## Bitcoin fees

Bitcoin fees depend on the transaction size and the network fee rate. The preview shows the fee and the fee rate.

## Biatec Safe costs

A new safe needs ALGO for its minimum balance, each asset opt-in and storage for proposals, about 2 ALGO for a safe that holds two assets and provides liquidity. Each proposal, approval and execution pays normal network fees.

## Running the AI

Biatec covers the cost of running the AI model. To keep the service fair, the backend has usage budgets and rate limits; in heavy load, guest questions may get shorter or cached answers.
