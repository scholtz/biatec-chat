# Fees and costs

Algorand network fees are small: the minimum is 0.001 ALGO per transaction, and a transaction group pays the sum of its transactions. Applications that call other applications may need extra fee budget.

Biatec DEX pools charge a trading fee set by the pool's fee tier; it is paid to liquidity providers. The exact tier is shown on the pool and in the preview of a swap before you confirm.

Biatec Chat does not charge for answering questions. Any action shows its fees and slippage in the preview before it is signed.
