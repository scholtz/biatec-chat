# DEX concepts

A DEX (decentralized exchange) lets you swap assets directly from your wallet without handing custody to anyone. Biatec DEX is a concentrated-liquidity automated market maker (AMM) on Algorand.

In a concentrated-liquidity pool, liquidity providers choose a price range for their liquidity. Liquidity inside the range earns trading fees, and capital is used more efficiently than in a classic constant-product pool. Each pool has a fee tier chosen when the pool is created.

Swapping trades one asset for another at the pool price. Slippage is the difference between the quoted price and the executed price; a slippage setting limits how much worse a price you accept. Adding liquidity deposits both assets of a pair into a range; removing liquidity withdraws them with earned fees.

Swaps and liquidity changes need an account that is signed in. The assistant can explain them to anyone.
