# Getting started

To explore, just ask the assistant: it explains Biatec products, Algorand and DEX concepts without an account.

To act, sign in. Select Sign in in the chat, finish the Google or Microsoft login in the popup, and your self-custody wallet is created for you by Biatec OIDC. After that you can ask for your addresses and balances, and to prepare transfers, swaps, liquidity changes and safes.

Every action shows a preview of exactly what will happen. Signing needs a one-time authorization per session, and larger amounts are approved on your phone with the Biatec authenticator. Fund your address with ALGO first, since every account needs a minimum balance.
