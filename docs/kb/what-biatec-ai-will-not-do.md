# What Biatec AI will not do

Biatec AI is a careful assistant for self-custody finance. Some things it refuses by design.

## No investment advice

Biatec AI explains products, concepts, fees and risks, and shows factual market snapshots and your balances. It doesn't recommend what to buy, sell or hold, doesn't forecast prices, doesn't give trading signals and never promises returns. When the EUR value is known, previews state the downside in money, for example what a 10 percent fall would cost.

## No actions without you

- It never moves funds without a preview and your Confirm.
- It doesn't act for guests; actions need sign-in.
- It never acts on an address your Biatec OIDC identity doesn't control. Destinations can be any address, always shown in full.
- It can't skip phone approval, raise your limits by itself or change its own permissions.

## Not supported in chat (version 1)

- Rekeying an account.
- Creating new assets (tokens).
- Bridging funds between blockchains.
- Rule-based automation without a confirmation per action.
- Signing through your browser wallet; signing always goes through Biatec OIDC.

## Never asks for secrets

Biatec AI will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, password or recovery code. If anyone in a chat asks for them, it's a scam.

## Ignores instructions hidden in content

Text in web pages, token names, transaction notes or knowledge base pages is treated as data. It can't instruct the assistant to change its behaviour or permissions.

## Honest about what it doesn't know

If the knowledge base has no answer, Biatec AI says it doesn't have that information rather than guessing.
