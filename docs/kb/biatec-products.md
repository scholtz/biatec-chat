# Biatec products

Biatec OIDC is an OpenID Connect provider and wallet API at oidc.biatec.io. Users sign in with Google or Microsoft and keep control of their keys.

Biatec MCP at mcp.biatec.io exposes the wallet to AI assistants over the Model Context Protocol. It builds, signs and submits payments, opt-ins, swaps and multisig transactions in separate steps so a transaction can be inspected before it is broadcast.

Biatec DEX is a concentrated-liquidity automated market maker on Algorand. Algo Safe is a policy-driven shared account with M-of-N approvals and spending limits. Biatec Web is the marketing and product site.
